The station clock struck six. Nora lifted the small case, and Alex reached for the door.

“Wait!” Nora called.

Alex turned. “What is it?”

“The ticket is still on the table.”

“Really?”

Nora held it up. “Really! You would have left it behind.”

Alex laughed and came back. Outside, a bicycle rolled down the slope toward the open gate.

“Stop!” Nora shouted.

Alex caught the handlebar before the bicycle reached the road. For a moment neither of them spoke.

“Are you sure the brake is on?” Nora asked.

“Yes. Everything’s okay.”

“Don't worry,” she said. “We still have time.”

They checked the door and walked toward the station together. The word wait appeared on a sign beside the crossing; neither of them read it aloud.
