ambiguous surface pronunciations, invalid/nonword and grammar-only entries;
exclusion of numeric-containing and kana-only surfaces; sorting and conversion
to a fixed-record binary index and UTF-8 key/katakana-pronunciation payload.

UniDic Consortium. Source-unique readings are not universally context-correct.
