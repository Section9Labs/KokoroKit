lexicon-es, lexicon-es-419, lexicon-es-AR, lexicon-it, lexicon-pt-BR,
lexicon-fr and lexicon-hi adapt pronunciations by Wiktionary contributors,
extracted by Tatu Ylonen and Wiktextract at Kaikki.org.
English Wiktionary website snapshot: 2026-10-03 / dump 2026-09-02.
Sources: https://kaikki.org/dictionary/ and https://kaikki.org/dictionary/rawdata.html
Attribution/history for each written form: https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/
followed by the percent-encoded UTF-8 form. Italian/Portuguese supplemental
form histories: https://it.wiktionary.org/wiki/ and https://pt.wiktionary.org/wiki/
Their exact snapshots/hashes and contribution counts are in the JSON manifests.

unsupported-symbol exclusions, removal of syllable dots/dental diacritics,
canonical affricates and broad-profile notation, preserved lexical stress,
Argentine sheismo/seseo, native Hindi conjunct conventions, sorted UTF-8 binary
index/payload packing. Mandarin stores numbered pinyin and excludes conflicting
readings. English-export keys with ambiguous readings cannot be replaced by a
convenient reading from a supplemental source.
