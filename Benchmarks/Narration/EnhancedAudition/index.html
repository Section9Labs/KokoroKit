What this comparison establishes

Basic uses the smaller Kokoro frontend; Enhanced uses FP32. Both use the same new pronunciation dictionaries and bounded decoder. These are controlled current-code comparisons, not exact recordings of TestFlight 123. The candidate adds length-indexed voice conditioning, dialogue-aware phrasing and small local pitch excursions. Model rate stays at 1×.

Cleanup options reuse identical generated audio. The lighter Spanish candidate is harmonicGentle; English uses gentle. No MP3/AAC compression, global time stretching or new training. Mac generation measurements do not establish iPhone memory, heat or locked-screen behavior. Choosing a better-sounding arm requires listening.
